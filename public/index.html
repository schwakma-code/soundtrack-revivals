How it's counted

  • The titles: for each year since 2007, the 10 films and the 10 scripted TV series with a season beginning that year that the most TMDb users have rated. — of the — titles have a soundtrack album on MusicBrainz; the rest can't be counted.
  • An older song came out at least 10 years before the film or TV season it plays in, by its earliest release on MusicBrainz. Score albums, remixes and singles are left out.
  • A revival: not in the Billboard Hot 100's top 50 (or the cutoff you pick) in the year before the release, then inside it in the 3 months (12 weeks) after. The Hot 100 data is every weekly chart since 1958, collected by a University of Texas course.
  • Music biopics are films about the artist: Bohemian Rhapsody and Michael. They are shown in teal, apart from songs placed in a scene.
  • Genres are the ones MusicBrainz users voted for most on the song, or on the artist when the song has none, grouped into broad families.
  • Each song card links to its own pages, and to Google Trends for its YouTube search interest around the release.
  • The Hot 100 is a US chart, and it began counting streams in 2012 and 2013, so earlier years are measured differently.
